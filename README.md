# Social-Engineering-Awareness
A cybersecurity awareness project about social engineering attacks, prevention and protection.
Introduction to Social Engineering
What is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is a type of cyberattack where criminals manipulate people into revealing confidential information, sharing passwords or performing actions that may compromise security.

Instead of attacking computer systems directly, attackers target human behaviour and trust.

Common Goals of Social Engineering
Stealing personal information.
Obtaining passwords and login credentials.
Accessing confidential company information.
Gaining unauthorised access to computer systems.
Committing financial fraud.
Why is Social Engineering Dangerous?

Social engineering is dangerous because it takes advantage of human emotions such as fear, curiosity, trust and urgency.

Even people with strong technical knowledge can become victims.

Conclusion

Understanding social engineering is important because awareness helps people recognise suspicious behaviour and protect themselves against manipulation.
